Virat Kohli was born on 5 November 1988 in Delhi, India. Raised in a middle‑class Punjabi family, he joined the West Delhi Cricket Academy at a young age. He made his international debut for India in 2008 and quickly gained a reputation as a world‑class batsman.

Over his career, Kohli has amassed thousands of runs across formats and set numerous batting records. Known for his aggressive style, fierce competitiveness, and consistency, he is widely regarded as one of the greatest cricketers of his generation. 

Beyond his on‑field success, Kohli’s work ethic, fitness regime, and leadership qualities have inspired a generation of young cricketers. His journey from a passionate youngster to a global sports icon demonstrates discipline, dedication, and unrelenting ambition.
